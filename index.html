I'm Ryan O'Grady, a 3D environment artist studying Game Art at Ringling College of Art & Design (class of 2026). I specialize in crafting believable, atmospheric game worlds — from damp stone dungeons to dense northeastern forests.

My process balances strong reference gathering, modular asset design, and thoughtful lighting to create spaces that feel lived-in and alive. I work primarily in Maya, Substance Painter, and Unreal Engine 5.

Currently seeking internship and junior artist opportunities in the games industry.
