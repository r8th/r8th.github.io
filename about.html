I am a recent graduate of Ringling College of Art & Design specializing in 3D environment art. I am passionate about creating immersive worlds that balance visual complexity, and environmental storytelling.

I enjoy every stage of the environment pipeline, from sculpting organic forms in Zbrush, and hard surface modeling in Maya, to developing procedural workflows for texturing, asset creation, and world building. My work combines artistic vision with technical problem solving to build engaging environments.

I am procifient in Unreal Engine 5, Maya, Zbrush, Substance Painter, Substance Designer, Adobe Photoshop, RizomUV, and Perforce revision control, with a working knowledge of Houdini and procedural content creation workflows.
